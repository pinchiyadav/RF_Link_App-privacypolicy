Privacy Policy – ITM P2P RF Path App

Last updated: February 2025

ITM P2P RF Path App (“the App”) is developed and maintained by Yogesh Yadav. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is collected, used, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Android Device Identifier (Android ID)

The App collects your device’s Android ID for the following purposes:

  • To verify your device for licensing
  • To unlock paid features after purchase
  • To prevent unauthorized sharing or cloning of licensed access

The Android ID is stored securely in Google Sheets using encrypted API access.

1.2 Google Play Billing Information

The App uses Google Play Billing for purchasing the paid license. No sensitive financial information (such as bank details, card numbers, UPI IDs, etc.) is ever accessed or stored.

Google only provides:

  • Purchase state (success/failure)
  • A transaction token needed to unlock the app

The App does not receive or store any payment credentials.

1.3 Optional User Messages

If you contact support from within the app, the following may be stored:

  • Your name
  • Your message or feedback
  • Your contact email (optional)

These messages are stored in Google Sheets only for support and communication.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Managing license activation
  • Restoring purchases
  • Responding to support queries
  • Improving App performance

No data is shared, sold, or used for advertising or tracking.

3. Data Storage & Security

Collected data is stored securely using:

  • Google Sheets API — protected by a private service account
  • Local encrypted storage on the device

All communication to Google APIs is encrypted using HTTPS.

4. Third-Party Services Used

The App uses the following Google services:

  • Google Play Billing — for secure purchase handling
  • Google Maps SDK — for map display
  • Google Elevation API — to fetch terrain elevation data
  • Google Sheets API — to store license status

These services may collect anonymized technical data as described in their own privacy policies.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. No information is knowingly collected from children.

6. Your Rights

You can request:

  • Deletion of your Android ID from our database
  • Removal of past support messages
  • Clarification about what data is stored
