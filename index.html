Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RF Link - Signal Strength, LOS (the "app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the app. It applies to the app on both Google Play (Android) and the Apple App Store (iOS). By using the app you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Summary

RF Link - Signal Strength, LOS is a radio propagation planning tool. The app:

  • May request your device location to set transmitter/receiver coordinates or to show maps;
  • Stores your input parameters (coordinates, frequency, heights, advanced settings) locally on the device so they are available when the app restarts;
  • Sends those parameters to a remote processing API operated by the app owner to run the propagation calculation, and returns results and plots;
  • Sends a per-device identifier to that same server to count your free monthly calculations, so the free trial works without you creating an account;
  • Can send user messages to a spreadsheet used as a simple inbox when you use the “Send Message” feature;
  • Offers optional in-app purchases — a one-time unlock and an auto-renewable monthly subscription — which are processed entirely by Apple or Google. We never receive or store your card or bank details.

2. Information We Collect

Location information

If you use features that require location (for example choosing transmitter or receiver positions on a map, or using device-based location input), the app will request location permission. Location is used only to populate coordinates and map display, and only while you are using the app. You may deny or revoke location permission at any time; denying permission will not prevent you from using the majority of app features (you can manually enter coordinates).

Input parameters

The app stores your radio planning inputs locally on the device (using SharedPreferences on Android and the equivalent local storage on iOS). This includes transmitter/receiver coordinates, frequency, antenna heights, advanced parameters, and terrain sampling preferences. These values remain on your device until you change or clear them.

Processing data sent to the cloud API

When you run a propagation calculation or generate a coverage map, the app sends the input parameters to a remote processing endpoint operated by the app owner. The data sent is the set of numeric parameters required for the radio model — coordinates, frequency, antenna heights, gains and losses. The server returns calculation results and, optionally, plot or heat-map images which the app displays. These calculation inputs are not stored after the result has been returned.

Device identifier and free-trial usage

The app allows a limited number of free calculations each month. So that this works without asking you to create an account or sign in, the app sends a per-device identifier to our server together with a count of how many free calculations have been used.

  • On iOS this is Apple’s identifierForVendor, an identifier scoped to this developer’s apps. It is reset if you delete all of our apps from the device.
  • On Android this is the Android ID, which is reset by a factory reset.

The record we store contains only this identifier, a usage count and the calendar month. It is not linked to your name, email address, Apple Account or Google Account, is not used for advertising, and is not used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. The counter resets each month.

Messages you submit

If you use the “Send Message” or feedback feature, your message is stored in a spreadsheet used by the app owner as an inbox. The message may include your name, subject, and contact email or phone number if you choose to provide them. These entries are visible to the app owner.

Technical and device information

The app may collect non-personal technical information required for normal operation (app version, operating system version, device model). The app does not collect analytics or crash logs unless you have explicitly enabled such a feature.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To perform radio propagation calculations and display results to you;
  • To pre-fill forms and restore the last-used parameters when you re-open the app;
  • To count free monthly calculations and to recognise a valid purchase or subscription;
  • To send your messages to the app owner’s inbox so they can respond;
  • To provide and improve the app’s features and fix bugs when you report them.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers. We may share information with:

  • Service providers that host or process your requests — principally Google Cloud Platform, which hosts the calculation API, the terrain data cache and the free-trial counter;
  • The elevation and mapping services used to obtain terrain data for the calculation;
  • Apple and Google, who process in-app purchases and tell the app whether a purchase is valid;
  • Law enforcement or government authorities when required by law.

5. Third-party services used

The app integrates with third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps SDK (Android and iOS) — used to render maps and map tiles. Google’s privacy policy applies to map usage: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Geolocation services — used to obtain device coordinates when you allow location access;
  • Google Cloud Platform — hosts the app owner’s backend, which performs the propagation and coverage calculations, holds the free-trial counter, and relays contact-form messages;
  • Apple App Store / Google Play billing — process in-app purchases and subscriptions. Their respective privacy policies apply to the payment itself: apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy

Messages you submit through the app are relayed to the owner’s inbox by the backend server; the app itself does not access the spreadsheet directly.

6. Payments and subscriptions

The app offers optional in-app purchases: a one-time lifetime unlock and an auto-renewable monthly subscription. Both unlock unlimited calculations; the app is otherwise usable with a limited number of free calculations per month.

All payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), depending on where you installed the app. We never receive, see, or store your payment card, UPI or bank details. The app receives only a confirmation from the store that a purchase is valid.

The app stores locally whether you hold an active purchase. On Android it also stores the purchase token needed to manage or cancel a subscription on your behalf.

Subscriptions renew automatically for the same price and period until cancelled. You can view, manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings (iOS) or Google Play subscriptions (Android). Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not retroactively refund the current period.

7. Data retention

  • Local input parameters and purchase status — stored on your device until you change them, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app.
  • Calculation inputs — not retained after the result is returned.
  • Free-trial records — the per-device identifier and usage count are retained while the app remains in use; the count resets each calendar month.
  • Messages you submit — retained in the owner’s inbox until deleted by the owner.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information on the device and in transit (all API calls use HTTPS). However, no system is completely secure — please avoid sending highly sensitive personal data through the app or in messages to the app owner.

9. Children

The app is a technical tool intended for a general and professional audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.
